# The README's reel (docs/gameplay.gif): the takes. tools/reel.py shoots this
# script, keeping every frame of each take (`rec start` .. `rec stop NAME`),
# and cuts the reel from them by its edit list (CUT: each shot's in and out
# ticks, how it hands over to the next). The takes run long on purpose: the
# edit chooses. In order, as a game goes: the title; a raccoon at its den by
# The Landing, parried and run through, and the den broken; a beaver's ambush
# from the water; a chameleon creeping up in Willow Hollow, turned and run
# through, and the map with one den crossed; the map again with five; the
# last one falling far off and THE ROT STIRS; into the arena, the giant toad
# rising out of the murk and croaking its name; its hop dodged, its tongue
# parried, the riposte, the killing blow, its death; the win clip. W, D and
# I set the scenes between the takes.
# The inputs are timed to the tick (the parries to the critters' and the
# toad's tells, which draw on the shared RNG): a change to any step count, or
# to the game's AI or timings, moves every later beat. Re-time from the
# changed take on with reel.py --sheets (each take's frames, numbered by tick).
# Takes are named, not numbered: CHGame's `chgame gif` (./ec gif) joins
# NN_name clips with hard cuts, and finding none it leaves the reel alone.
# (`ec check` runs this script like every script, at rec's EVERY.)
