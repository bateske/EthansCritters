# Play (M3+M4): A on the title starts the squire at the hut; he walks out
# of The Landing on foot (the camera following, the world streamed every
# frame), wades the shallows (slower), stops at deep water and at the
# willows, thrusts and guards facing both ways, and the SELECT overlay
# shows the frame stats. Then warps (W x y, his feet in world px) to the

release

release
