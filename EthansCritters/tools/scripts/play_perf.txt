# What a play frame costs: the squire walks across the swamp with the camera
# panning every way, then thrusts and guards (new frames for the cache),
# then the pacing under an added load (J: microseconds after gfx_wait()).
# `step` draws a frame per logic tick, as the board does while frames fit
# (`wait` would catch up three ticks a frame). For the board's card timing:
#   CHSD_SIM_CMD_US=832 CHSD_SIM_BLK_US=222 ec run tools/scripts/play_perf.txt out/play_perf
# perf: rnd = the average from gfx_wait() to the flush, max = the worst, us.
# Z: PLAY (frames drawn, at 30 fps, over the 7.9 ms budget, mode switches,
# the mode now, average and worst us after gfx_wait(), card commands and
# blocks) | SPOOL (the frame cache's fetches, commands, blocks, misses,
# deferred, misses at draw time, its worst fetch).
wait 30
tap A                   # the squire at the hut's door
step 4
say Z0
perf start              # (the counters start here)
hold RIGHT
step 240                # east along the trodden path
hold UP
step 200                # north across the meadow
hold RIGHT
step 200                # east along the beaver pond's north shore, wading
hold DOWN+RIGHT
step 100                # into the pond until the deep water stops him
hold DOWN
step 100                # (still stopped: the camera stands)
hold LEFT
step 200                # west through the shallows past the lodge
hold UP
step 200                # north again
hold LEFT
step 150
release
perf walk
say Z
snap walk_end
say Z0
perf start
hold A                  # thrust right
step 1
release
step 24
hold LEFT
step 2
hold A                  # thrust left
step 1
release
step 24
hold B                  # guard
step 30
release
step 4
hold RIGHT+A            # a thrust on the move
step 1
hold RIGHT
step 30
hold DOWN
step 30
release
step 10
perf moves
say Z
snap moves_end
# The pacing: 5 ms more work a frame puts the world's 3.5-4.6 ms over the
# 7.9 ms budget, so the screen drops to 30 fps (a frame every second tick)
# after 4 of 8 frames; without it, back to 60 after ~2 s under 6.9 ms.
say Z0
say J 5000
hold RIGHT
step 120
release
perf loaded
say Z
snap at30
say Z0
say J 0
hold LEFT
step 200
release
perf unloaded
say Z
snap back60
