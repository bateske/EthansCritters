# The whole exploration loop (M6b): all six nests cleared, one after the
# other, with I1 (no harm) and warps (W puts the squire just west of each,
# facing it; the cleared ones stay cleared). Each is knocked down to its
# last HP with D and falls to a real thrust: the stinger, the banner, the
# white mushroom, its pip on the HUD, the save. The compass points to the
# nearest nest still standing; Willow Hollow's mood lifts with its brood.
# After the sixth: THE ROT STIRS (a rumble, a shake), and the compass
# turns ochre and points to The Rot's gate; at the gate, that board gone,
# its brambles part (gate.txt has it beat by beat).
#   CHSD_SIM_CMD_US=832 CHSD_SIM_BLK_US=222 ec run tools/scripts/full_loop.txt out/full_loop
