# The card screen. With the card in, this pulls it out (X0) under the
# title: the next frame's stream fails, and the game says INSERT CARD; put
# back (X1), A tries again and the title is back. Run with no card at all
# (--card naming a missing file), every snap is the card screen.
wait 30

snap nocard
say X1
tap A
wait 10
snap back
