# The regions' moods (M6b): the palette mixed toward a colour, eased in
# over about a second as the squire crosses into a region. The Landing,
# Reed Shallows and Beaver Dam have none; Willow Hollow is a little dimmer
# and greener (until its brood is cleared), The Rot a dark olive (until
# its toad is beaten: boss_fight.txt). A warp (W) puts a region's mood on at once, so
# the crossings are walked. A hurt's red screen (gfx_setFade) and the
# death's grey go over the mood. I1 keeps the squire on his feet among
# the critters on the way.
#   CHSD_SIM_CMD_US=832 CHSD_SIM_BLK_US=222 ec run tools/scripts/moods.txt out/moods
wait 30
say W 160 626           # The Landing, by the hut: no mood
say I1
step 30
snap landing
say W 196 304           # Reed Shallows, the turtle's sandbar: none
step 30
snap shallows
say W 500 340           # Beaver Dam's north bank: none ...
step 20
snap dam
hold UP                 # ... north into Willow Hollow: its mood eases in
step 40
release
snap hollow_in          # half way
step 40
snap hollow             # dimmer and greener
say W 604 212           # Willow Hollow's east end: east through the gap in the brambles
step 10
hold RIGHT
step 40
release
snap rot_in             # into The Rot: the Hollow's green eases out, then the olive in
step 90
snap rot                # The Rot: a dark olive
say H 2                 # a hurt: the red screen over the mood
step 4
snap rot_hurt
step 30
snap rot_after          # the red gone, the olive stays
say D 4                 # the chameleon brood falls: Willow Hollow's mood is lifted
say W 500 280
step 30
snap hollow_lifted
say A
