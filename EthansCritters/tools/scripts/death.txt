# Dying and getting up (M5; the death clip since M7b). By the raccoon den:
# the far raccoon den is knocked down (D5: cleared nests stay cleared, and
# it is saved), he takes a hit (H4), eats the red mushroom by the bushes
# (the 48 come back when he dies), and the raccoons finish him (H10b: ten
# half hearts from behind). The death row, the colour drains away, then the
# death clip (Path F, its own dusk palette): THE SWAMP KEEPS YOU, the
# critters edging in on him out of the dark, held; A: GET UP once it has
# played, and A stands him up at the hut with full hearts, the den's pip
# still filled, every mushroom back and every critter gone, in the swamp's
# own colours again. A prints the actors and nests.
#   CHSD_SIM_CMD_US=832 CHSD_SIM_BLK_US=222 ec run tools/scripts/death.txt out/death

step 4
