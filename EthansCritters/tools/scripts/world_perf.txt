# What the world costs a frame: a walk across the swamp with the camera
# panning in every direction, the world streamed from the card each frame
# (one read of 16 or 17 column blocks, each copied into the framebuffer as
# the next arrives). For board-like numbers give the simulator the card the
# board measured:
#   CHSD_SIM_CMD_US=832 CHSD_SIM_BLK_US=222 ec run tools/scripts/world_perf.txt out/world_perf
# (perf: rnd = the average from gfx_wait() to the flush, max = the worst, us.)
wait 30
tap A
wait 4
say W 84 680            # the south-west corner
wait 2
perf start              # (the counters start here)
hold RIGHT
wait 240                # east along the south meadows
hold UP+RIGHT
wait 120
hold UP
wait 160                # north past the dam
hold LEFT
wait 200                # west along the dam's north bank
hold UP+LEFT
wait 100
hold LEFT
wait 120
release
perf pan
say W 636 142           # Willow Hollow, then still: the camera stops
wait 60
perf still
say W 776 344           # through the gate into The Rot
hold UP
wait 60
hold RIGHT
wait 120
release
perf rot
snap rot_end
